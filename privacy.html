Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · MS Life Assistant · Valmont

We designed MS Life Assistant to help users organize their own observations. This notice describes the data the app handles and how we use service providers.

Information you enter

Daily energy ratings, symptom observations, brain-fog ratings, sleep entries, priorities, text notes and appointment questions are stored locally in encrypted device storage. We do not operate a user-account or cloud synchronization service for these entries. The app does not send this personal journal content to our servers.

Voice input

If you choose voice dictation, your microphone and speech recognition permissions are requested. Speech recognition may use the operating-system provider's services under that provider's privacy practices. You may use typed input instead. The app saves the reviewed transcription to local device storage.

Subscriptions

Apple processes in-app purchases and payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and determine subscription access. In doing so, RevenueCat and Apple process purchase receipts, subscription status, product identifiers and app-level identifiers needed to provide and restore entitlements. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Sharing and exports

When you choose to export notes or share an appointment summary, you direct the app to create a copy and use your device's sharing tools. The recipient and destination are under your control; such copies may be governed by the receiving service's privacy practices.

Data retention and control

Journal records persist on your device until you delete them through the app or remove the app, subject to your device's backup settings. The app offers local export and deletion controls. Purchase information may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their respective legal and operational requirements.

Children and health advice

This is a self-management aid and not an emergency or clinical monitoring service. It does not diagnose MS or predict relapses. If you experience new or worsening neurological symptoms, contact a healthcare professional.

Security

Private local notes use platform-backed encrypted storage. No technology is risk-free; protect your device and review where you share exports.

Contact

Questions about data or privacy: valmontmarketing@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
