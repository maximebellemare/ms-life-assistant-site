Support

For help with MS Life Assistant, technical problems, data export, subscriptions or accessibility, email valmontmarketing@gmail.com.

Subscription and trial questions

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Visit iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions to manage or cancel. Trial eligibility is determined by Apple. To recover an eligible existing subscription, open MS Life Assistant and choose Restore purchases on the paywall.

My notes and check-ins

Information entered into the app is kept in encrypted device storage. In the app, open Settings to export or delete local records. Exporting or sharing a doctor-visit PDF creates a copy outside the app that you control.

Voice capture

Voice transcription needs your permission and may involve services provided by the device operating system. You can always type instead.

Important safety note

This app is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Contact your care team for any new or worsening neurological symptoms. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.
